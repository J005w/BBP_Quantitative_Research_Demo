# Bounded real-market study: qualification decision

Decision on 19 October 2026: **not executed**. Two official data candidates were checked before downloading candles or examining outcomes. No return experiment, market observations, market-data checksums or statistical findings are invented here. This is a feasibility record and prospective next phase, not an empirical result or a completed experiment.

| Candidate checked | Specific evidence | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Binance Vision spot BTCUSDT 5-minute archives | Official [dataset terms](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/blob/master/TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md) separately govern data and derived analytics under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 and restrict commercial uses. Personal non-production research is allowed. Official [schema](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/blob/master/README.md) describes spot trade klines and a timestamp-unit change in 2025. | No blanket assertion that a MIT-licensed software project inherits data rights. More decisively, BTC/USDT trade OHLC is neither USD Bid OHLC nor a qualified CFD contract. The existing engine explicitly requires the latter. Relabelling it would fabricate compatibility. |
| Kraken public BTC/USD OHLC endpoint | Official [API documentation](https://docs.kraken.com/api-reference/market-data/get-ohlc-data) limits retrieval to the latest 720 rows and includes a forming last candle. | At M5 this yields at most 2.5 days, insufficient for the source default 499-H4 warmup (~83 days). Historical archive rights and sufficient completed history would need separate confirmation; the endpoint also supplies no historical executable Bid/Ask path or observed bar-arrival series. |

The exact missing evidence is a rights-compatible historical archive with enough warmup, qualified UTC/session/timestamp units, explicit trade-versus-quote price semantics, contract/account-currency mapping, and an honest execution/cost specification. Quote/arrival evidence is required for an execution-parity claim. A clearly labelled trade-price proxy study could be useful later with a narrow adapter and newly frozen assumptions; it must not be passed through the current Bid-qualified input check unchanged. Shorting/financing assumptions for spot also differ from the demo's linear CFD scenario.
